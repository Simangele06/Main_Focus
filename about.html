About Us

Main Focus was created to make sure the boy child is never left behind. I started this business because i wanted to provide greate clothing specifically for boys, from when they are still in their mothers womb up to young kids.

We made sure our clothes are very affordable, but still made with strong, tough material that can handle everyday wear and tear. Because the quality is so great, these clothes can even be passed to the next generation meaning the baby can wear the clothes and can be passed to the next child.

We also know that after giving birth, things can be overwhelming, and sometimes you feel like you lose a piece of who you used to be while trying to adjust to motherhood. That is another big reason why Main Focus was created. We wanted to make things just a little bit easier for you, giving you quick and free access to your boy's clothes online so you do not have to stress and helping you fell supported every step of the way.

More than just clothes, Main Focus is here to help children make memories. We want kids to look back at their old pictures one day and remember their favorite outfits, like a cool Spider Man t-shirt. Main Focus is here to stay, making sure parents always have a place to rely on.

Mission

Our mission at Main Focus is to provide strong, high quality clothes that does not tear easily when kids are running around or playing hard. We are her to make sure parents can easily get affordable, tough clothing for their boys without any stress, so they always know they have a place to rely on.

Vision

In the future i want Main Focus to be a trusted, household clothing brand for boys that goes way beyond what it is now. My big dream is to grow it into an international business with physical shops all over the world.

I see Main Focus collaborating with bog brands like Nike, Adidas and Woolworths as well as working with DC and Marvel. Kids love watching Marvel and DC characters and they get inspired by heroes like Spider Man and Captain America, bit right now parents often have hard time finding those clothes and end up having to pay people just to print them. I want Main Focus to be the place where parents can easily come and get those exact styles.

My vision is for Main Focus to be the go to name everywhere so that whenever someone thinks about buying clothes for a boy, they instantly remember that they can always rely on Main Focus.
